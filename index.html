How to play

It takes a minute a week. The hard part is getting it right.

HDA

1. Make your picks

Choose a home win, draw or away win for every fixture in the round. You can change your mind right up until kick-off.

J

2. Play your Joker

Put your Joker on the match you think will have the most goals. You get a bonus point for every goal scored, whatever the result.

5

3. Watch it live

Live scores appear next to each fixture with the points you stand to win. Climb the round table, the season standings and the Medals Table.

Scoring

Back the underdog and you could land a huge score.

Every correct pick scores 1 to 5 points

A pick's value depends on how everyone else has called the match. The fewer players who pick a result, the more it's worth. Points move as predictions come in and are fixed once the game kicks off.

The rules in brief

  • One pick per fixture: home, draw or away.
  • One Joker per round: 1 point per goal in that match.
  • Change picks any time before kick-off.
  • Rounds follow each week's league fixtures.
  • Medals go to the top three in every round.
  • A warning triangle reminds you of any blank picks or a missing Joker.

Private leagues and Superleague

Play everyone, and your mates.

👥 Private leagues

Create your own league in any competition and invite friends, family or the office. The tables and stats show just your league's members, and you still play against everyone else too. Switch leagues with one tap at the top of the screen.

⚔️ Superleague

A round-robin inside your private league. Each round you're drawn against another player. A correct pick scores a goal if your opponent got it wrong, and the most goals wins. Results go into a league table with 3 points for a win and 1 for a draw. The season lasts long enough for everyone to play everyone once.
